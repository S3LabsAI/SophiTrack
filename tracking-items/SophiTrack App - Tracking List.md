# SophiTrack App - Tracking List

## 1. Diapers (log for each 24-hour period)

- **Wet diapers**: total count in last 24 hrs
- **Poopy diapers**: total count in last 24 hrs
- **Poop color**: e.g., black/tarry (meconium), dark green, mustard yellow, brown, or anything unusual (red, white, chalky)
- **Poop consistency**: e.g., sticky/tarry, seedy, pasty, runny, formed, hard
- **Time of each change** (helpful for spotting patterns)

## 2. Bottle-Feeding

- **Time of each feed**
- **Amount per feed**: rough estimate is fine (e.g., 1–2 oz, 2–3 oz)
- **Frequency**: number of feeds in last 24 hrs
- **Total intake in 24 hrs**: sum of the above
- **Type of milk**: formula vs. pumped breast milk (including colostrum)

## 3. Pumping (if you start)

- **Time of each session**
- **Frequency**: sessions per 24 hrs
- **Duration**: minutes per session
- **Volume collected**: oz or mL per session (and per side, if you want detail)
- **Total volume in 24 hrs**

## 4. Optional Extras

- Notes on spit-up, fussiness, or feeding difficulty
- Questions to bring to the next appointment
